AI and Scam Calls

AI makes it possible to create deep-fakes, like representations of real people or things that have happened in real life. Anyone who spends time on the Internet knows that there is a huge amount of images, audio and video freely accessible. While some uses of deep-fakes are done for innocent fun, others can amount to spreading false information in the middle of a tight political race. In business deep-fakes can trick people into believing that a product or service has the ability of a particular person or company. Deep-fakes can be used to commit fraud or make other deceitful representations made for illegal and dishonest purposes. It is also possible that deep-fakes become so common that people doubt the legitimacy of all such people or products.

My opinion on AI is that it is not the greatest thing for the world. It could be good if used correctly, but most of the time it is used for bad, like reasons shown above. All in all, Ai is not good because of all of the downsides it has.
